Billing issues may include invoice questions, subscription fees, plan charges, payment failures, declined cards, or checkout problems.

If a user asks about a charge, they should review the charge date, amount, invoice, subscription status, and recent account activity.

If a payment fails, the user should check whether the card was declined, the transaction failed, or the checkout process did not complete.

Some billing issues may require support review if the amount is unexpected, the charge cannot be identified, or the payment failure continues.

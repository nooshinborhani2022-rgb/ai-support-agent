Refund and charge-related issues may include refund requests, duplicate charges, completed charges, recent payments, or money-back requests.

If a user wants a refund, they should open the relevant order or billing record and submit a refund request from the account dashboard or support section.

If a user was charged twice, they should compare the dates and amounts in their billing history to confirm whether the same payment appears more than once.

Duplicate charges and refund requests may require support review if the billing history shows repeated or unexpected transactions.

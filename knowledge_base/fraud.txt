Fraud and security issues may include unauthorized charges, suspicious payments, stolen card reports, account compromise, or activity the user says was not theirs.

If a user says someone used their card or a charge is not theirs, the issue should be treated as high risk and escalated to a security or support specialist.

The user should secure their account or payment method if they suspect unauthorized access.

Security-related issues should not be handled as ordinary billing questions because they may involve fraud or account compromise.
